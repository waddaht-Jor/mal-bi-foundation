CERTIFIED ENTERPRISE DEFINITION

30-day transacting customers · 128,420

A distinct, non-test customer with at least one settled customer-initiated monetary transaction in the trailing 30 calendar days, measured at 23:59 GST on the reporting date. Reversals, failed events, fees, interest postings and internal transfers are excluded.

COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) WHERE settled_at > as_of_date - 30 days AND is_qualifying_activity = TRUE
